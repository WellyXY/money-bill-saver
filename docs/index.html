Illustrated walkthrough
Sample data only · no account connection

Step 1 of 5 · 0–10 seconds

Choose what the review can see.

Give Codex the bills you supply or explicitly authorize. Name the account and date range when reviewing a mailbox.

Installing the skill does not grant access to email, bank accounts or other services.

Step 2 of 5 · 10–20 seconds

Keep each kind of evidence separate.

A settled receipt, prepaid top-up and user-named service say different things. The skill traces dates and gaps before calculating costs.

A top-up is not automatically a monthly subscription or a refundable charge.

Step 3 of 5 · 20–30 seconds

See the complete inventory.

The report keeps observed, uncertain and user-named services together. Prices without evidence remain unknown.

The known monthly baseline is partial. It excludes unknown prices and usage.

Step 4 of 5 · 30–40 seconds

Turn signals into precise questions.

Possible overlap and unknown usage lead to checks. A separate refund case needs transaction and eligibility evidence.

A review lead does not mean a refund is available or approved.

Step 5 of 5 · 40–50 seconds

Decide what happens next.

Open the evidence, check the account, and use a local support draft if it fits. Requests and account changes stay under your control.

Copying a draft does not send it. Verify details before contacting a merchant.
