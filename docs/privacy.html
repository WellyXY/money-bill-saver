Money Bill Saver

Privacy notice

Last updated September 16, 2026.

Money Bill Saver reviews billing evidence that you choose to provide or authorize your assistant to read. Bills, account details, and the resulting audit can be sensitive. Keep real reports private.

What the developer receives

The published HTML tutorial uses fictional data and has no account connection, form, analytics, tracker, or developer-operated backend. The bundled helper scripts make no network requests. The developer does not receive your bills or generated audit through this tutorial or those scripts.

What your host may process

When you use the skill in ChatGPT, Codex, or another host, evidence you provide to that host may be processed under that host's terms and privacy policy. If you authorize a mail, file, or other connector, its provider and the host may process retrieved content. Installing the skill alone does not grant account or mailbox access.

Local output and sharing

The skill creates local audit files, including a self-contained webpage and supporting data. The webpage does not automatically send its contents to the developer. You control where you save or share it. Do not post real bills, account identifiers, or private reports in public GitHub Issues.

Support

For questions, open a GitHub Issue without including private billing records. Content you voluntarily post there is handled by GitHub under its own policies.
