Refund questions

Screening leads come first so possible waste is visible. Specific claims and existing refund follow-ups remain separately counted.

A reason to check is the first step. Overlap or missing activity evidence can justify a review. A past-charge request also needs the payment, charged period, usage context and applicable refund conditions.

How refund requests are assessed
  1. Find a lead: similar workflows, a trial that may have become paid, or an old account record with current usage unknown. Missing update emails alone do not establish non-use or continued billing.
  2. Confirm what happened: match the account and settled charge; check actual use or the user's dated non-use statement, cancellation, stored data and dependencies.
  3. Choose a remedy: a supported billing correction or policy-based request, a clearly attributed goodwill request, or a future cancellation/downgrade. Check the seller, purchase channel and relevant dates before drafting a claim. Approval is not guaranteed.
